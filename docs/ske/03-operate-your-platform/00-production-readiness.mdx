Before opening your platform to developers, check that it can provision services and that
your team can monitor and recover it. This guide covers the checks to make after installing SKE.

## Prerequisites

- SKE installed through the [Operator guide](/ske/installing-ske/install-with-operator).
- A list of your Destinations, StateStores, GitOps agents and optional integrations.
- Access to Kubernetes resource status, events and logs.
- A Promise you can use to test provisioning on your platform.

## Review the platform

1. Record explicit component versions and check [compatibility requirements](/ske/releases/compatibility).
   Review the [platform architecture](/ske/reference/parts-of-a-platform) against your deployment.
   Check that worker GitOps agents are running. If you use Compound Promises or receive
   resource requests through Git, also check the GitOps agent on the platform cluster.
2. Verify image pull, release-storage and StateStore credentials. Record ownership, expiry and
   rotation procedures for all referenced Secrets. Keep registry, release-storage and TLS Secrets
   required by the Operator in `kratix-platform-system`.
3. Confirm certificate renewal for webhooks and metrics. If cert-manager is disabled, supply the
   [custom certificates](/ske/installing-ske/install-with-operator#custom-certs), including the
   Platform Manager certificate where required. Check TLS trust for each external service.
4. Review [resource requests and limits](/ske/installing-ske/install-with-operator#resources)
   and scheduling settings against observed workload demand and cluster policies.
   Check that cluster policies allow workflow Jobs and integration Jobs to run and pull their images.
5. Decide how to collect controller and workflow logs, Kubernetes events, metrics and traces.
   Configure [Kratix observability](/main/reference/observability) and choose workflow Job retention
   long enough to investigate failures. Assign owners for alerts and incident response.
6. Configure [health checks](/ske/guides/healthchecks) where needed. Check that the GitOps agent
   or [bucket HealthSource](/ske/reference/healthsource) brings `HealthRecord` objects onto the
   platform cluster and that the resource request's health status updates.
7. Rehearse [backup and restore](/ske/installing-ske/backup-and-restore), including external
   stores and provisioned service data. Record escalation channels from the [support policy](/ske/support).

## Verify the developer journey

Confirm platform Deployments are ready and the Operator reports the intended version.
Then install your test Promise and submit a resource request in a namespace developers can use.
Follow the request through each stage:

1. Check that the Resource Configure Workflow completes successfully.
2. Find its generated files in the StateStore, under the target Destination's path.
3. For Kubernetes workloads, check that the worker GitOps agent applies the files.
4. Check that the provisioned service works as expected.
5. If health checks are enabled, confirm the request's health status reflects the latest check.

If you use a [portal integration](/ske/integrations/portal-integrations/intro), repeat the test
with a developer account: find the Promise in the portal, submit a request and check its status.
Check access permissions and any required approval. Record the results and who to contact if a check fails.

## Troubleshooting and next steps

Use [Troubleshoot SKE](/ske/operations/troubleshooting) when checks fail. Once the checks pass,
give developers access according to your platform's access policy. Repeat the provisioning test
after [upgrades](/ske/installing-ske/upgrades).
