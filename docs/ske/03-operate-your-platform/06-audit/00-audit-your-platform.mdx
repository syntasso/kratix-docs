SKE records the person who changes each resource request, so you can see who
uses your platform and what they did. It records them in two places:

- **On the request**, in `kratix.io/last-change*` annotations. These show who
  made the latest change to a request.
- **In the Kubernetes audit log**, as an audit annotation on every create,
  update and delete. Use it to see the full history of a request, or to count
  the people using your platform over time.

## Where to start

- To see what SKE records, and to choose which part of a person's identity it
  records, read
  [Recording who changed a request](/ske/operations/audit/requester).
- To record people who use a portal such as Backstage or Cortex, read
  [Recording ownership from a portal](/ske/integrations/portal-controller/request-ownership).
- To turn on the audit log, find it, and count the people using your platform,
  read the guide for your cluster:
  - [Amazon EKS](/ske/operations/audit/eks)
