Use this guide to review an installed SKE platform before developers depend on it.
The result is a verified platform configuration and an operational runbook for your topology.

## Prerequisites

- SKE installed through the [Operator guide](/ske/installing-ske/install-with-operator).
- An inventory of Destinations, StateStores, GitOps agents and optional integrations.
- Access to workload status, events and logs, and a representative Promise for verification.

## Review the platform

1. Record explicit component versions and check [compatibility requirements](/ske/releases/compatibility).
   Review the [platform architecture](/ske/reference/parts-of-a-platform) against your deployment.
   Confirm worker GitOps agents and any platform GitOps agent required by Compound Promises or request ingestion.
2. Verify image pull, release-storage and StateStore credentials. Record ownership, expiry and
   rotation procedures for all referenced Secrets. Keep registry, release-storage and TLS Secrets
   required by the Operator in `kratix-platform-system`.
3. Confirm certificate renewal for webhooks and metrics. If cert-manager is disabled, supply the
   [custom certificates](/ske/installing-ske/install-with-operator#custom-certs), including the
   Platform Manager certificate where required. Check TLS trust for each external service.
4. Review [resource requests and limits](/ske/installing-ske/install-with-operator#resources)
   and scheduling settings against observed workload demand and cluster policies.
   Check that policies admit workflow Jobs, integration Jobs and their images.
5. Decide how to collect controller and workflow logs, Kubernetes events, metrics and traces.
   Configure [Kratix observability](/main/reference/observability) and choose workflow Job retention
   long enough to investigate failures. Assign owners for alerts and incident response.
6. Configure [health checks](/ske/guides/healthchecks) where needed. Verify that records return
   through your selected GitOps or [bucket HealthSource](/ske/reference/healthsource) path.
7. Rehearse [backup and restore](/ske/installing-ske/backup-and-restore), including external
   stores and provisioned service data. Record escalation channels from the [support policy](/ske/support).

## Verify the developer journey

Confirm platform Deployments are ready and the Operator reports the intended version.
Install the representative Promise, submit a request in an authorised namespace and verify
its workflow, scheduled output, Destination reconciliation and service behaviour.
Confirm health records arrive if enabled.

For [portal integrations](/ske/integrations/portal-integrations/intro), also verify discovery,
permissions, request delivery and status with a developer account. Include approval in the check
if requests require review. Record the evidence and owners in your runbook.

## Troubleshooting and next steps

Use [Troubleshoot SKE](/ske/operations/troubleshooting) when checks fail. Once the review passes,
open access according to your platform's access policy and use the same representative journey
after [upgrades](/ske/installing-ske/upgrades).
